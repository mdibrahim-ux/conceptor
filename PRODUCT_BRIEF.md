# CONCEPTOR — Product Brief

**One-liner:** A visual-first learning workspace that helps students understand textbook concepts through appropriate visual explanations and interactive explorations, then find those resources again instantly.

**Audience:** University students learning from textbooks, PDFs and lecture notes.

**Primary pains:** Text-heavy AI reorganisation does not always improve understanding; conversation history makes previously useful explanations difficult to retrieve.

**Primary pillars:**
1. Contextual visual explanations — static diagrams, flowcharts, interactive models or step-through animation depending on the concept.
2. Concept-centred navigation — chapters and topics, not chat history, organise the learning journey.
3. Persistent visual library — save, search and reopen explanations, diagrams, notes and quizzes.

**Required Track 4 outputs:** Notes and quizzes remain first-class resources within the workspace.

**Prototype sample:** Operating Systems — Process Management.

**Prototype limits:** Prepared sample content; local interactions; no live AI/PDF extraction/backend.
